FAQ

Questions,
answered

Still confused? Open a ticket in our Discord server.

How long does the application take?

Usually between 24 and 48 hours. If you don't hear back within 72 hours, feel free to open a ticket.

Do I need to pay anything to join?

Never. FAMILIES GANG is completely free. Anyone asking for money is scamming you.

Can I apply again if I get declined?

Yes, but wait at least 14 days and fix whatever was missing in your first application.

Is a microphone really required?

Yes. Voice comms are essential for our operations and for building real trust inside the family.
